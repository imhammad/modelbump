\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We replicated the finding that most open-source projects leave a retired LLM only after it has been shut down, on \MigrationsAway{} newly mined migration commits with a measured label. The direction holds, but the share is about \AfterLate\%, not \KimAfterShutdown\%, under every definition we tried. The typical migration is a few days late, projects mostly follow the provider's recommended line but often pick a model that will soon be retired as well, and few migrations come with test or evaluation changes. The gap between a deprecation notice and the projects that depend on the model is real, and it is where tools for detecting retired models and checking a migration's behaviour can help.
