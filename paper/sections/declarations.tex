\bmhead{Author contributions} The author designed the study, directed and checked the code, built the dataset, labelled the samples and wrote the paper with the AI assistance described above.

\bmhead{Data availability} The dataset, the hand-labelled samples and the counts behind every number are available on Zenodo \citep{hassan2026dataset} at \DataDOI. The code that builds the dataset, this paper's numbers, tables and figures, and the paper itself is available at \RepoURL.
